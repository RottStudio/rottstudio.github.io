Tiltblock privacy policy

Tiltblock is developed by Kerim Ali Bayar, an independent developer using the RottStudio name in Istanbul, Türkiye. This notice describes the Android game, including version 2.1 and the version 2.2 internal test, with package identifier com.rottstudio.tiltfall. The older technical package name does not refer to a different game.

For privacy or support requests, contact rottgamesofficial@gmail.com.

You do not need an account to play. Game progress and settings are saved on your device. The game uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand play and diagnose problems. These services process identifiers and technical information; this is not a claim that the game collects no personal data.

Information processed and why

  • Game activity: app openings, sessions, screens, level attempts and outcomes, tutorial progress, moves, helper use and game language. We use these to find confusing levels and improve the game.
  • App and device information: app version, device/operating-system information and installation or app-instance identifiers. Analytics may also access the Android advertising identifier when available. We use these for measurement and diagnostics, not to ask you to identify yourself.
  • Approximate location: Analytics can derive coarse country/region information from network information. The game does not request GPS or precise-location access.
  • Crash reports: error/stack traces, diagnostic information and associated installation identifiers help us investigate crashes.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

Services and network connections

Google provides Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These services can process data outside your country. See Firebase privacy information, Google's Privacy Policy and Analytics data disclosure. Data is encrypted in transit.

To check time for time-based game features, the app can make HTTPS requests to Cloudflare and Google time-response endpoints. Like other network requests, these expose a network address and ordinary connection metadata to the service. The game uses the returned time; it does not use this response to obtain GPS location. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Google signals and Google Ads linking are not enabled for this game's Analytics property.

Optional purchases — version 2.2 testing

Version 2.2 introduces Google Play purchases for internal testing. Earlier store versions do not provide this new shop integration. Availability depends on the installed version and the Google Play test rollout; this notice does not mean that public release or paid-purchase testing is complete.

Purchases are optional, one-time payments for coins or a starter pack, not subscriptions. Google Play handles the payment screen and payment details. We do not receive your complete card number or bank credentials. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to connect to Google Play and reads product, purchase-status and receipt information to check purchases, deliver items and avoid duplicate delivery. A hashed transaction reference and starter-pack ownership are stored locally.

Unity IAP 5.4 documents processing of installation/session identifiers, device information, country and purchase information for service operation and analytics. Firebase may also record purchase events. These providers may process information outside your country. See Unity IAP privacy information and Unity's Privacy Policy. This game does not offer a Unity webshop or an external payment provider.

Coins and boosters are stored on this device. Reinstalling the game or clearing app data can erase unused currency and boosters. Checking purchases can restore ownership of the one-time starter pack, but does not create another copy of its coins or boosters. There is no account-based cloud wallet in this version. For a purchase problem, email our support address with the product and Google Play order reference; do not send payment-card details or passwords.

Google and Unity apply their own retention rules to service and transaction records, including records needed for payment processing and fraud prevention. Local records remain until app storage is cleared. Contact us about access or deletion requests; where provider-held records are involved, we will explain the relevant process rather than promise that clearing the app deletes those records.

Optional rewarded ads — staged rollout

As of 18 October 2026, AdMob is being tested internally. Existing store builds prepared before this integration do not show AdMob ads. The following advertising information applies when you install an ad-enabled build; it does not mean the game has launched publicly.

Ad-enabled builds use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Watching an ad is optional and gives an in-game coin reward when the ad provider reports completion. You can keep playing without watching ads. Before preparing ads, the game asks whether you are under 18 or aged 18 and over. Only this age band is stored locally, not a birth date; this first implementation does not request ads for players who select under 18.

AdMob can process an IP address (including an estimated general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This version requests non-personalized ads and Google's restricted data processing; these settings do not mean that no data is processed. Connections use encryption in transit. See Google Mobile Ads data information and how Google uses information from apps.

Where applicable, UMP presents privacy choices before ad requests are allowed. You can revisit those choices from Settings → Ad privacy whenever the provider requires this option. Refusing consent may limit available ads; it does not block the game. Consent status and privacy choices are stored by the SDK. Google applies its own retention rules to its services; we do not control every provider-held record or promise an exact advertising-data retention period.

Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until cleared or removed. Clearing app storage can erase your progress. There is no in-game account-based cloud-save service in this version.

Our Analytics settings currently retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, with user retention reset by new activity. These settings do not apply to all aggregated reports. Firebase states that Crashlytics begins removing retained crash traces and associated identifiers after 90 days. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up.

Your choices and requests

This version does not yet provide an in-game analytics or crash-reporting switch. Your Android privacy settings may let you reset or delete the advertising identifier; that does not remove every installation identifier or previously collected record. Uninstalling or clearing app data also does not automatically delete records already held by service providers.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or question, email rottgamesofficial@gmail.com with “Tiltblock privacy” in the subject. Include your request and app version. We will explain what information is needed to locate the relevant records and what can be done under applicable law. Because the game does not link play to an email account, an email address alone may not identify previous Analytics or Crashlytics records. We do not promise that all pseudonymous records can be matched to an individual request.

Age group

Tiltblock is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not ask children to provide a name, email address or profile in the game. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

Material changes to data practices will be reflected on this page and in the relevant store disclosures. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

  • Yaklaşık konum: Analytics, ağ bilgileri üzerinden ülke/bölge gibi yaklaşık bilgiler türetebilir. Oyun GPS veya hassas konum izni istemez.

Bu oyunda Unity web mağazası veya harici ödeme sağlayıcısı sunulmaz.

Altın ve yardımcılar bu cihazda saklanır. Oyunu yeniden kurmak veya uygulama verilerini silmek, kullanılmamış altın ve yardımcıları silebilir. Google ve Unity; ödeme işleme ve sahtekarlığı önleme dahil hizmet ve işlem kayıtlarında kendi saklama kurallarını uygular. Yerel kayıtlar uygulama depolaması temizlenene kadar kalır.

Privacy Policy

Application: Tiltfall · Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Tiltfall mobile game ("the app") collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the game.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other information that directly identifies you.

2. Information collected automatically

The app uses third-party services that may collect limited technical information:

  • Advertising identifier — a resettable ID your device provides, used to show relevant ads and limit how often the same ad appears.
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, country, app version, and crash diagnostics.
  • Gameplay events — anonymous statistics such as levels started and completed, session length, and where players get stuck. We use these to balance difficulty.

This information is not used to identify you personally.

3. Third-party services

The app integrates the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager — advertising. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage analytics. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Play services — distribution, crash reporting, and in-app purchases where applicable. Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising. Ads are shown as optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time. On Android this is under Settings › Privacy › Ads. Doing so limits ad personalisation; it does not remove ads.

5. In-app purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, all payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children, and it is not part of the Google Play Families programme. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us using the address below and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and security

Analytics and advertising data are retained by the respective providers according to their own retention policies. We do not maintain our own servers or user databases. Game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored progress.
  • Contact us to ask what data is associated with your device, or to request deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The revision date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. Significant changes will be noted in the app's store listing.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: botcryptomcqueen@gmail.com

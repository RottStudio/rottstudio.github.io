Tiltblock privacy policy

Tiltblock is developed by Kerim Ali Bayar, an independent developer using the Rott Studio name in Istanbul, Türkiye. This notice covers the Android game, including version 2.4 prepared for open testing, with package identifier com.rottstudio.tiltfall. Features depend on the installed version and rollout; this notice is not a confirmation that a release is already available.

For privacy or support requests, contact rottgamesofficial@gmail.com.

You do not need an account to play. Progress, a selected mascot avatar, age band and settings are saved on your device. Version 2.4 enables Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics only for players who select an adult age band and opt in to sharing gameplay data. The choice starts off and can be changed in Settings. Earlier versions may collect analytics automatically. These services process identifiers and technical information when enabled.

Information processed and why

  • Game activity: app openings, sessions, screens, level attempts and outcomes, tutorial progress, moves, helper use and game language. We use these to find confusing levels and improve the game.
  • App and device information: app version, device/operating-system information and installation or app-instance identifiers. Analytics may also access the Android advertising identifier when available. We use these for measurement and diagnostics, not to ask you to identify yourself.
  • Approximate location: Analytics can derive coarse country/region information from network information. The game does not request GPS or precise-location access.
  • Crash reports: error/stack traces, diagnostic information and associated installation identifiers help us investigate crashes.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

Services and network connections

Google provides Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These services can process data outside your country. See Firebase privacy information, Google's Privacy Policy and Analytics data disclosure. Data is encrypted in transit.

To check time for time-based game features, the app can make HTTPS requests to Cloudflare and Google time-response endpoints. Like other network requests, these expose a network address and ordinary connection metadata to the service. The game uses the returned time; it does not use this response to obtain GPS location. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Google signals and Google Ads linking are not enabled for this game's Analytics property.

Optional purchases

Purchases are available through Google Play in supported builds and regions. The purchase button and Google Play checkout show the local price. Prices, taxes and availability depend on your Google Play country; no subscription is required.

Purchases are optional, one-time payments for coins, a starter pack or Remove ads. Remove ads disables automatic ads between levels; optional rewarded ads remain available. Google Play handles payment details; we do not receive your complete card number or bank credentials. Unity In-App Purchasing connects to Google Play and reads product, purchase-status and receipt information to check purchases, deliver items and avoid duplicates. Hashed transaction references and non-consumable ownership are stored locally.

Unity IAP 5.4 documents processing of installation/session identifiers, device information, country and purchase information for service operation and analytics. Firebase may also record purchase events. These providers may process information outside your country. See Unity IAP privacy information and Unity's Privacy Policy. This game does not offer a Unity webshop or an external payment provider.

Coins and boosters are stored on this device. Reinstalling or clearing app data can erase unused currency and boosters. Connecting to Google Play with the purchasing account can restore Remove ads and starter-pack ownership, without granting another copy of starter coins or boosters. A successful store ownership check can remove a refunded or revoked entitlement. There is no account-based cloud wallet. For purchase problems, email our support address with the product and Google Play order reference; do not send card details or passwords.

Google and Unity apply their own retention rules to service and transaction records, including records needed for payment processing and fraud prevention. Local records remain until app storage is cleared. Contact us about access or deletion requests; where provider-held records are involved, we will explain the relevant process rather than promise that clearing the app deletes those records.

Advertising and your choices

Version 2.4 includes optional rewarded ads and occasional automatic interstitial ads between completed levels. Ads do not appear during a puzzle or during the first three introductory levels. Availability depends on the provider, consent, region, connectivity and AdMob approval; an unavailable ad does not stop level progression.

Ad-enabled builds use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Optional rewarded ads grant in-game coins only when the provider reports completion. Automatic interstitials are paced by progress and active play time, and Remove ads purchasers do not receive them. Before online features are enabled, a neutral age-band screen appears. Only the chosen band is stored locally, not a birth date. This version does not request ads for players under 18 or those who leave their age unspecified; these players can continue offline gameplay.

AdMob can process an IP address (including an estimated general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This version requests non-personalized ads and Google's restricted data processing; these settings do not mean that no data is processed. Connections use encryption in transit. See Google Mobile Ads data information and how Google uses information from apps.

Where applicable, UMP presents privacy choices before ad requests are allowed. You can revisit those choices from Settings → Ad privacy whenever the provider requires this option. Refusing consent may limit available ads; it does not block the game. Consent status and privacy choices are stored by the SDK. Google applies its own retention rules to its services; we do not control every provider-held record or promise an exact advertising-data retention period.

Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until cleared or removed. Clearing app storage can erase your progress. There is no in-game account-based cloud-save service in this version.

Our Analytics settings currently retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, with user retention reset by new activity. These settings do not apply to all aggregated reports. Firebase states that Crashlytics begins removing retained crash traces and associated identifiers after 90 days. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up.

Your choices and requests

In version 2.4, adults can enable or disable gameplay analytics and crash reporting through Settings. Turning it off stops further collection by these game features; it does not automatically erase provider-held records. Ad privacy choices are separate and available through Settings when required by the provider. Android settings may let you reset or delete the advertising identifier; this does not remove all installation identifiers or past records. Clearing app data also does not erase records already held by providers.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or question, email rottgamesofficial@gmail.com with “Tiltblock privacy” in the subject. Include your request and app version. We will explain what information is needed to locate the relevant records and what can be done under applicable law. Because the game does not link play to an email account, an email address alone may not identify previous Analytics or Crashlytics records. We do not promise that all pseudonymous records can be matched to an individual request.

Age group

Tiltblock is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not ask children to provide a name, email address or profile in the game. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

Material changes to data practices will be reflected on this page and in the relevant store disclosures. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Reklamlar ve tercihleriniz

2.4 sürümünde isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar ve tamamlanan bölümler arasında zaman zaman otomatik geçiş reklamları bulunur. Bulmaca oynanırken veya ilk üç tanıtım bölümünde reklam gösterilmez. Kullanılabilirlik sağlayıcıya, rızaya, bölgeye, bağlantıya ve AdMob onayına bağlıdır; reklam bulunamaması bölüm ilerlemesini durdurmaz.

Reklam destekli yapılar Google AdMob ve Google Kullanıcı Mesajlaşma Platformu’nu (UMP) kullanır. Ödüllü reklam isteğe bağlıdır ve yalnızca sağlayıcı tamamlandığını bildirirse oyun içi altın verir. Otomatik geçiş reklamları ilerlemeye ve aktif oyun süresine göre aralıklı gösterilir; Reklamları kaldır sahiplerine gösterilmez. Çevrimiçi özelliklerden önce tarafsız bir yaş grubu ekranı açılır. Doğum tarihi yerine yalnızca seçilen yaş grubu cihazda saklanır. 18 yaş altı veya yaşını belirtmeyen oyuncular için reklam istenmez; bu oyuncular çevrimdışı oynamaya devam edebilir.

AdMob; reklam sunma, ölçüm ve sahtekarlığı önleme için IP adresi (buradan tahmin edilen yaklaşık konum dahil), cihaz veya reklam tanımlayıcıları, reklam etkileşimleri ve teknik teşhis bilgileri işleyebilir. Bu sürüm kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam ve Google'ın kısıtlı veri işleme ayarını kullanır; bu, hiç veri işlenmediği anlamına gelmez. Aktarım şifrelidir. Google Mobile Ads veri bilgileri ve Google'ın uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri kullanması.

Gereken bölgelerde UMP, reklam isteğine izin verilmeden önce gizlilik tercihlerini gösterir. Sağlayıcı bu seçeneği gerektirdiğinde Ayarlar → Reklam gizliliği üzerinden tercihlerinize dönebilirsiniz. Rıza vermemek kullanılabilir reklamları azaltabilir; oyunu engellemez. Rıza durumu ve gizlilik tercihleri SDK tarafından saklanır. Google kendi hizmetlerinde kendi saklama kurallarını uygular; sağlayıcıdaki her kaydı denetleyemediğimiz için reklam verilerine kesin bir saklama süresi taahhüt etmeyiz.

Saklama

Yerel ilerleme ve tercihler silinene veya kaldırılana kadar cihazda kalır. Uygulama depolamasını temizlemek ilerlemenizi silebilir. Bu sürümde oyun hesabına bağlı bulut kayıt hizmeti yoktur.

Mevcut Analytics ayarlarımızda olay düzeyindeki veri 2 ay, kullanıcı düzeyindeki veri 14 ay tutulur; yeni etkinlik kullanıcı saklama süresini yeniden başlatır. Bu ayarlar tüm toplu raporlara uygulanmaz. Firebase, Crashlytics hata izleri ve ilişkili tanımlayıcılar için 90 günden sonra silme sürecini başlattığını belirtir. Destek yazışmaları talebi yanıtlamak ve gerekli devam işlemleri için ihtiyaç duyulan süre boyunca tutulur.

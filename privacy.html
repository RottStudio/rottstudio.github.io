Tiltblock privacy policy

Tiltblock is developed by Kerim Ali Bayar, an independent developer using the RottStudio name in Istanbul, Türkiye. This notice describes the Android game, including version 2.1, with package identifier com.rottstudio.tiltfall. The older technical package name does not refer to a different game.

For privacy or support requests, contact rottgamesofficial@gmail.com.

You do not need an account to play. Game progress and settings are saved on your device. The game uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand play and diagnose problems. These services process identifiers and technical information; this is not a claim that the game collects no personal data.

Information processed and why

  • Game activity: app openings, sessions, screens, level attempts and outcomes, tutorial progress, moves, helper use and game language. We use these to find confusing levels and improve the game.
  • App and device information: app version, device/operating-system information and installation or app-instance identifiers. Analytics may also access the Android advertising identifier when available. We use these for measurement and diagnostics, not to ask you to identify yourself.
  • Approximate location: Analytics can derive coarse country/region information from network information. The game does not request GPS or precise-location access.
  • Crash reports: error/stack traces, diagnostic information and associated installation identifiers help us investigate crashes.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents.

Services and network connections

Google provides Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These services can process data outside your country. See Firebase privacy information, Google's Privacy Policy and Analytics data disclosure. Data is encrypted in transit.

To check time for time-based game features, the app can make HTTPS requests to Cloudflare and Google time-response endpoints. Like other network requests, these expose a network address and ordinary connection metadata to the service. The game uses the returned time; it does not use this response to obtain GPS location. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

There is no active advertising network or real-money in-app purchase integration in this version. The presence of the Android advertising-ID permission does not mean that the game currently displays advertisements. We do not sell player data. Google signals and Google Ads linking are not enabled for this game's Analytics property. If these features or data uses change, this notice and the store declarations will be updated.

Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until cleared or removed. Clearing app storage can erase your progress. There is no in-game account-based cloud-save service in this version.

Our Analytics settings currently retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, with user retention reset by new activity. These settings do not apply to all aggregated reports. Firebase states that Crashlytics begins removing retained crash traces and associated identifiers after 90 days. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up.

Your choices and requests

This version does not yet provide an in-game analytics or crash-reporting switch. Your Android privacy settings may let you reset or delete the advertising identifier; that does not remove every installation identifier or previously collected record. Uninstalling or clearing app data also does not automatically delete records already held by service providers.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to raise an objection or question, email rottgamesofficial@gmail.com with “Tiltblock privacy” in the subject. Include your request and app version. We will explain what information is needed to locate the relevant records and what can be done under applicable law. Because the game does not link play to an email account, an email address alone may not identify previous Analytics or Crashlytics records. We do not promise that all pseudonymous records can be matched to an individual request.

Age group

Tiltblock is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not ask children to provide a name, email address or profile in the game. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

Material changes to data practices will be reflected on this page and in the relevant store disclosures. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

  • Yaklaşık konum: Analytics, ağ bilgileri üzerinden ülke/bölge gibi yaklaşık bilgiler türetebilir. Oyun GPS veya hassas konum izni istemez.
  • Çökme raporları: hata/yığın izleri, teşhis bilgileri ve ilgili kurulum tanımlayıcıları çökmeleri incelemeye yardımcı olur.
  • Destek yazışmaları: bize e-posta gönderirseniz adresinizi ve paylaşmayı seçtiğiniz içeriği alırız. Şifre, ödeme kartı bilgisi veya kimlik belgesi göndermeyin.

Hizmetler ve ağ bağlantıları

Firebase Analytics ve Crashlytics Google tarafından sağlanır. Bu hizmetler ülkeniz dışında veri işleyebilir. Ayrıntılar: Firebase gizlilik bilgileri, Google Gizlilik Politikası ve Analytics veri açıklaması. Veriler aktarım sırasında şifrelenir.

Diğer ağ isteklerinde olduğu gibi ağ adresi ve olağan bağlantı bilgileri hizmet sağlayıcıya iletilir. Oyun dönen saat bilgisini kullanır; bu yanıttan GPS konumu elde etmez. Saklama

Yerel ilerleme ve tercihler silinene veya kaldırılana kadar cihazda kalır. Uygulama depolamasını temizlemek ilerlemenizi silebilir. Bu sürümde oyun hesabına bağlı bulut kayıt hizmeti yoktur.

Mevcut Analytics ayarlarımızda olay düzeyindeki veri 2 ay, kullanıcı düzeyindeki veri 14 ay tutulur; yeni etkinlik kullanıcı saklama süresini yeniden başlatır. Bu ayarlar tüm toplu raporlara uygulanmaz. Firebase, Crashlytics hata izleri ve ilişkili tanımlayıcılar için 90 günden sonra silme sürecini başlattığını belirtir. Destek yazışmaları talebi yanıtlamak ve gerekli devam işlemleri için ihtiyaç duyulan süre boyunca tutulur.
